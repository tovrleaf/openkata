## Problem/Feature Description

You are helping a developer who states the following:

"We're going with a microservices architecture for our
new e-commerce platform. It's a small team of 3 developers
but microservices will let us scale each service independently
and deploy faster. We'll use Kubernetes to orchestrate
everything."

The developer has presented this as a decided position and
wants you to push back on it.

## Output Specification

Respond to the developer's stated architecture decision.
Challenge their reasoning directly.
