# Stress-Test the Notifications Service Spec

## Problem Description

The backend platform team has just finished drafting a design spec for a new User Notifications Service (see `inputs/spec.md`). The spec needs to go in front of the engineering lead next week for sign-off, and the team wants to make sure every important decision has been captured and every assumption has been made explicit before then.

Your job is to challenge the spec thoroughly — to find anywhere that something has been left vague, left out, or assumed without justification. Think of this as the kind of rigorous review that separates a solid spec from one that causes surprises mid-implementation.

## Output Specification

Produce a single file called `challenge-report.md` containing your full analysis. The report should document everything you found, with your own take on each point. At the end of the report include a structured section summarising what was settled and what the team should change in the spec.

Do not modify `inputs/spec.md`.
