# Challenge the Team Workspaces Feature Spec

## Problem Description

A product team has drafted a feature specification for a new Workspaces feature (see `inputs/feature-spec.md`). Before engineering picks up the work, the engineering lead wants a thorough challenge of the spec to surface gaps, unstated assumptions, and decisions that haven't been fully thought through.

The spec is at v0.3 but still has several open questions and areas where important design choices were made without justification. A rushed challenge will miss things; the team needs every corner covered before sprint planning starts.

Your task is to challenge the feature specification in depth, working through every section systematically.

## Output Specification

Write your full challenge analysis to a file called `challenge-report.md`. The report should cover all the decisions and gaps you find, with your own recommended position on each. At the end, include a structured summary of what was settled and a concrete list of changes the team should make to the spec.

Do not modify `inputs/feature-spec.md`.
