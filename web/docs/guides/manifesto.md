# Manifesto

The manifesto explains why OpenKata exists. It lives in the root
of the repository, so it is the first thing a contributor finds,
and it is the only copy. This page points to it.

[Read the manifesto](https://github.com/tovrleaf/openkata/blob/main/MANIFESTO.md)
on GitHub.

It covers three things:

- **The shift.** Agents continue the move to higher abstraction,
  so what was implicit craft has to become codified practice.
- **The problem.** Most AI-assisted development is ad hoc. Good
  patterns are discovered and lost, and bad ones are repeated.
- **The response.** Codified practices that can be taught,
  shared and reused, guided by three principles: humans govern
  and agents execute, portable by default, and explain the why.
